## Concepts used, restatements, gaps and notes

### revenue

_component_guard 0.5 (ADR-061): a candidate worth less than half of the largest candidate for the same year is a component (General Mills' `Revenues` was $2.0B against $19B total in 2019-2024, Hershey's $1.5B, Camden's fee line $0.01B), so it is skipped. Companies switch tags over time (GOOGL, TSLA, DUK). Banks tag total net revenue as Revenues (JPM). SalesRevenueGoodsNet is last so it only fills years nothing else covers (KO 2007-2017). The two lease-income concepts (ADR-064) are the last resort for REITs that tag no `Revenues` (Camden Property Trust: `RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax` is a $0.01B non-lease component and rental income is $1.57B); the guard above skips the component, and lease income is within a few percent of total revenue for the REITs that report both._
